FAQ

Is the football widget really free?

Yes, completely. No signup, no API key. The only condition is to keep the widget as it is, with its link.

Where do the probabilities come from?

From AI Football Predictor, which computes the win, draw and loss probability of every match. Each probability is locked before kickoff, then checked against the real result, publicly.

How do I add the widget to WordPress?

Add a “Custom HTML” block to your page or post, paste the two lines of code and publish. It also works in a sidebar widget.

Will the widget slow down my site?

No. It is lazy loaded in a separate frame, after the rest of your page.

Can I show only one club or one competition?

Yes. Pick a competition (for example the Premier League) or type a club name: the code updates on its own.

Are the matches updated automatically?

Yes. The widget always shows the next matches, with nothing to do.

Is it betting advice?

No. These are statistical probabilities, for information only. 18+ only.
