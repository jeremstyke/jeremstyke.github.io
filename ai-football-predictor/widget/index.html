A free football probabilities widget for your site

Show your readers the win probability of the next matches. Free, updated on its own, two lines of code.

Copy this code where you want the widget

<div class="afp-widget" data-lang="en" data-n="6"></div>
<script async src="https://jeremstyke.github.io/ai-football-predictor/embed.js"></script>

Options

data-langfr or en (default: the visitor's language)
data-lgcompetitions, e.g. F1,CL (Ligue 1 and Champions League). Empty = all
data-nnumber of matches, 1 to 20 (default 6)
data-themedark or light

Competition codes

E0 Premier League · F1 Ligue 1 · SP1 LaLiga · I1 Serie A · D1 Bundesliga · CL Champions League · P1 Liga Portugal · N1 Eredivisie · SC0 Scotland · B1 Belgium · T1 Turkey · G1 Greece · BRA Brazil · ARG Argentina · NOR Norway · ROU Romania · SA Saudi Arabia · HR Croatia · RS Serbia

Is it really free?

Yes. The only condition is to keep the widget as it is, with its links.

Where do the probabilities come from?

From AI Football Predictor. Every result is checked publicly on the reliability page.

Is it betting advice?

No. These are probabilities for information, not certainty. 18+.
