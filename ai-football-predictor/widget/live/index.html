FAQ

Is the live football widget free?

Yes, completely. No signup, no API key. The only condition is to keep the widget as it is, with its link.

Which matches are shown live?

Every current match in 29 competitions (Premier League, LaLiga, Champions League…). Matches where a team has at least a 65% chance to win are highlighted in green. You can filter by competition or club.

How does the probability move during the match?

It starts from our pre-match expected goals, scaled to the time left, then added to the current score. A goal or the minutes going by make it move.

How often is the score updated?

Data is refreshed every few minutes, and the match clock keeps running inside the widget.

How do I add the live widget to WordPress?

Add a “Custom HTML” block, paste the two lines of code and publish. It also works in a sidebar.

Will the widget slow down my site?

No. It is lazy loaded in a separate frame.

Is it betting advice?

No. These are statistical probabilities, for information only. 18+ only.
