How the AI picks its predictions

01
📥

Data in

Results, goals, home and away form, league tables and bookmaker averages for every match in more than 20 competitions.

02
🧮

Model

An attack and defence rating for each team, weighted towards recent matches, gives expected goals (xG) and a Poisson model turns them into home, draw and away probabilities.

03
🎯

Selection

Only matches where one outcome reaches the confidence threshold are kept. No pick when the data is not convincing, and the bot says so.

04
🔒

Lock and verify

Picks are locked 3 hours before kick-off and every result is published on the channel, wins and losses, so the record can be checked.

Glossary

xG (expected goals)
How many goals a team should score on average in this match, according to the model.
1X2 probabilities
The chance of a home win (1), a draw (X) and an away win (2). They add up to 100 %.
Over 2.5
The probability that the match has 3 goals or more.
Hit rate
Correct picks divided by settled picks.
Simulated profit
What 100 staked on every pick at the average bookmaker odds would have returned. A statistic, not advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is AI Football Predictor free?

Yes. Today's predictions and every analysed match are free for subscribers of the Telegram channel.

How accurate are the predictions?

The live hit rate is shown at the top of this page and in the app, computed on every settled pick. No model wins every time: the record shows wins and losses.

Which leagues are covered?

Premier League, Ligue 1, LaLiga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Champions League and many other leagues and cups.

Why are there days with no prediction?

The model only keeps matches where it is confident enough. When no match reaches the threshold, the bot shows the matches analysed and explains why none was kept.

Is this betting advice?

No. These are statistical estimates for information only. Football is uncertain and past results do not guarantee future ones. 18+ only if you bet, and only with money you can afford to lose.

How do I start?

Open the Telegram bot, join the channel and the predictions open in the mini app.
