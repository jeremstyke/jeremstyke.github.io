AI Football Predictor

The win probability of every football match: the most likely matches of the day, live, team and competition sheets, and the proof that our probabilities are right.

What is AI Football Predictor?

A free app that gives the win, draw and loss probability of every football match in more than 25 competitions, with expected goals, form and live updates.

Are the probabilities reliable?

Every probability is locked before kickoff and checked against the real result. The Reliability tab shows what we announced and what happened, only on our own matches since launch.

Is it betting advice?

No. These are probabilities for information, not certainty. 18+.
