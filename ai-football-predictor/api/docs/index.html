AI Football Predictor API

Win, draw and loss probabilities, expected goals and most likely score for every football match in more than 25 competitions, plus AI-written match previews in JSON or ready-to-publish HTML. Every probability is locked before kickoff and checked against the result.

Authentication

Send your key in the x-api-key header (recommended), or as ?key= in the URL. Keep server-side keys on your server: anyone who sees a key can use its quota. Every successful answer has a meta object with your plan and calls_left_today. Quotas reset at midnight UTC (calls) and on the 1st of the month (previews).

GET/v1/matches

Matches with their probabilities. Without a date: from 3 hours ago to 3 days ahead.

ParameterDescription
dateOne day, YYYY-MM-DD (UTC).
from, toISO dates for a custom window.
competitionCodes, comma separated: E0,CL,F1. See competitions.
teamPart of a team name: Arsenal.
min_probabilityOnly matches where one outcome reaches this value, for example 0.65.
limit1 to 500, default 100.
{
  "data": [{
    "id": 52,
    "kickoff": "2026-11-08T20:00:00+00:00",
    "competition": { "code": "SP1", "name": "LaLiga" },
    "home": "Ath Madrid", "away": "Barcelona",
    "probabilities": { "home": 0.29, "draw": 0.23, "away": 0.48 },
    "most_likely": "away",
    "expected_goals": { "home": 1.31, "away": 1.82 },
    "most_likely_score": { "score": "1-2", "probability": 0.09 },
    "over_2_5_goals": 0.6,
    "both_teams_score": 0.6,
    "locked": true,
    "result": null,
    "preview_image": "https://txvrlvcxfnylnrpdrxtt.supabase.co/functions/v1/afp-og?n=52"
  }],
  "count": 1,
  "meta": { "plan": "free", "calls_left_today": 99, "attribution": { "source": "AI Football Predictor", "url": "https://jeremstyke.github.io/ai-football-predictor/" } }
}

locked becomes true 3 hours before kickoff: the probability no longer changes. After the match, result gives the score, the outcome and most_likely_happened.

GET/v1/matches/{id}

One match, same fields.

GET/v1/previews

The latest match previews (title, standfirst, image, link), all plans. lang: en (default) or fr. limit: 1 to 50.

GET/v1/previews/{id}

The full preview of a match (Content and Pro plans, counts as one preview in your monthly quota). {id} is the match id.

ParameterDescription
langen or fr.
formatjson (default: title, lede, body paragraphs, image, url and ready html) or html (only the article, ready to paste).
curl -H "x-api-key: YOUR_KEY" \
  "https://txvrlvcxfnylnrpdrxtt.supabase.co/functions/v1/afp-api/v1/previews/52?lang=fr&format=html"

Previews are written by our AI from our own data only (probabilities, expected goals, form, standings) and updated with the result after the match. Previews exist for the big matches.

GET/v1/reliability

No key needed. How often the most likely outcome happened and the Brier score, for every match since launch and for the 65%+ matches. Small samples move a lot: judge over hundreds of matches.

GET/v1/competitions

No key needed. The competition codes: E0 Premier League, CL Champions League, SP1 LaLiga, I1 Serie A, D1 Bundesliga, F1 Ligue 1, P1 Liga Portugal, N1 Eredivisie, SC0 Scottish Premiership and more.
