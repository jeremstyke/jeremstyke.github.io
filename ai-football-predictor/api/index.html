Football probabilities API: win, draw, loss, xG and live for your apps

In short

Our API automatically sends your website or app the win, draw and loss probability of every match (more than 100 matches a week) in 29 competitions, with expected goals, most likely score, live probabilities and simulated end-of-season tables. Every probability is locked before kickoff and publicly checked.

✅ The API is open

Probabilities and previews in JSON or HTML. Free key in 10 seconds, paid plans for more calls and full previews.

What you get

GET /matchesUpcoming matches with 1 / X / 2 probabilities, expected goals, most likely score, over 2.5 goals. Filters: competition, date, team.
GET /liveLive matches: score, minute, scorers, stats, and the probability as it moves.
GET /tablesPredicted end-of-season table: expected points, title, top 4 and relegation chances (20,000 simulations every night).
GET /teams/{id}One team: form, next matches with probabilities, season projection.
GET /reliabilityOur track record: every locked probability and the real result, to check our reliability.
GET /articlesReady-to-publish pre-match articles written by our AI from our data: title, standfirst, full text in English and French, image with both club logos. Updated with the result after the match.

Why us

Checkable by anyone

Every probability is published and frozen 3 h before kickoff, then compared with the real result. Nothing is deleted, anyone can check, and the data is downloadable.

Honest

We do not claim to beat the bookmakers. We provide serious, up-to-date probabilities whose reliability is checked in public.

Ready to plug in

No need to build or maintain a model: you get the numbers and display them. A few lines of code are enough.

Wide coverage

29 competitions: the top 5 leagues, the Champions League, national cups and rarer leagues.

More than odds

Expected goals, the most likely score, chances that move during the match and the likely end-of-season table.
