How to set your wallpaper

iPhone

Save the image to Photos, open it, tap Share, then "Use as Wallpaper".

Android

Open the image in Gallery or Photos, tap the menu, then "Set as wallpaper".

Chrome new tab

Open a new tab, click "Customize Chrome" (bottom right), then "Upload an image" and pick any 4K wallpaper.

Windows / Mac

Right-click the 4K image and choose "Set as desktop background" (Windows) or use System Settings, Wallpaper (Mac).
