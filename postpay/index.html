How PostPay works

Three steps, all inside Telegram. No website account, no invoice, no waiting for a payout.

1

Owners list their channel

Add @Postpayadsbot as admin, set a price per post and how many ads you accept per day. Our team reviews each channel within 48h.

2

Advertisers book a post

Browse channels by topic, subscribers and price. Send your ad with your own button. The owner approves it.

3

Pay in USDT, it goes live

Payment goes straight to the owner's wallet (90%) and PostPay (10%). The bot publishes the ad for 24h and reports the clicks.

Frequently asked questions

What is PostPay?

PostPay is a Telegram bot and mini app where advertisers buy ads in Telegram channels and channel owners sell them. Prices are fixed by each owner and paid in USDT on the TON blockchain.

How much does PostPay take?

PostPay takes a 10% fee. The channel owner receives 90% of the price of every ad, directly in their own wallet, in the same transaction.

Does PostPay hold my money?

No. PostPay is non-custodial: the advertiser's payment goes straight from their wallet to the channel owner's wallet. PostPay never holds funds.

Is there a minimum number of subscribers?

No. Small channels are welcome. The maximum price per ad depends on the size of the channel, from 5 USDT under 500 subscribers up to 5,000 USDT above 500,000.

How do I add my channel?

Open @Postpayadsbot, tap List my channel and add the bot as an admin with the right to post messages. Your channel is reviewed by our team within 48 hours, then shown in the catalog.

Do I approve every ad?

Yes. Each ad request is sent to you first. You approve or refuse it. Only approved ads can be paid and published.
