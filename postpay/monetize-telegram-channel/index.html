How to monetize your Telegram channel in 2026

You built an audience on Telegram. Here are the real ways to earn money from it, what each one requires, and how to sell your ad space without chasing advertisers one by one.

1. Sell ad posts directly

Selling posts is the most common way channels make money. An advertiser pays you to publish their message, usually for 24 hours, sometimes with a button to their bot, channel or website. It works for any topic and any size, as long as your audience is real and active.

The hard part is not the post itself. It is finding advertisers, agreeing on a price, getting paid safely and making sure everyone keeps their word. That is exactly what a marketplace handles for you.

2. Telegram's official ads

Telegram runs its own ad platform and shares part of the revenue with eligible public channels. It is passive, but you do not choose the ads, eligibility rules apply, and income depends on Telegram's own campaigns.

3. Paid content and subscriptions

Some channels sell access to a private channel, premium posts or Stars-based content. It can pay well for expert niches (trading, education, tools), but you need content people are ready to pay for every month.

4. Affiliate links

You recommend a product and earn a commission on sales or sign-ups. It fits review and deal channels. Too many affiliate posts can make your audience leave, so most channels mix it with other income.

It depends on your subscribers, your topic and how many ads you accept. On PostPay, the maximum price per ad is set by channel size, from 5 USDT under 500 subscribers to 5,000 USDT above 500,000. See the full table and an earnings calculator on the Telegram ad prices page.

Tips to sell more ads

  • Keep a regular posting rhythm: advertisers look for active channels.
  • Choose a fair price. Starting lower helps you get your first ads and reviews.
  • Share your personal PostPay link in your channel bio so advertisers can book you directly.
  • Refuse ads that do not fit your audience. Trust is what makes your channel worth paying for.
