How to buy a Telegram ad

  1. Open @Postpayadsbot and browse the catalog. Sort channels by subscribers or price and filter by topic and language.
  2. Pick a channel and send your ad: text, or a photo or video with a caption. It will be posted exactly as you send it.
  3. Add your button: the text (for example Join now) and the link to your channel, bot or site.
  4. Wait for approval. The channel owner accepts or refuses your ad.
  5. Pay in USDT from your TON wallet. The ad goes live automatically and stays 24 hours.
  6. Get your results: clicks and cost per click when the ad ends.

What you need

  • A TON wallet, for example Tonkeeper or Telegram's Wallet (TON Space)
  • Enough USDT on TON for the ad price
  • About 0.1 TON for network fees

Why buy Telegram ads on PostPay

Tips for a good Telegram ad

  • Pick channels whose topic matches your offer, more than the biggest ones.
  • Keep it short: one clear promise and one button.
  • Use a strong image or short video.
  • Test several small channels, then buy again on those with the best cost per click.

Scams, fake giveaways, adult content and requests for seed phrases are refused automatically.
