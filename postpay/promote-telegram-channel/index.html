How to promote a Telegram channel

Real subscribers come from people who already read channels like yours. Here are the methods that work, from free to paid, and the ones to avoid.

📢 Find channels to advertise in

1. Buy a post in channels on the same topic

It is the fastest way to reach readers who are already interested in your subject. A crypto channel grows with posts in other crypto channels, a recipe channel with posts in food channels. On PostPay you pick a channel, send your ad with a button to your channel, and it is posted for 24 hours. At the end you see how many people clicked.

2. Swap posts with channels of the same size

Cross-promotion is free: two owners publish a short post about each other's channel. It works best between channels of similar size and close topics, so both sides gain.

3. Publish content people forward

Every forwarded post shows your channel name with a link. Short guides, useful lists, exclusive news and good visuals are forwarded the most. Keep a regular rhythm so new readers see a living channel.

4. Share your link where you already have an audience

  • Your website, newsletter and email signature
  • Your other social networks: bio, stories, pinned posts
  • Your app or bot, with a "Join our channel" button
  • Groups where self-promotion is allowed, without spamming

5. Use shareable folders

Telegram lets you share a folder of channels with a single link. Build a folder with channels on your topic, with their owners' agreement, and share it together: each channel brings readers to the others.

6. Make the first visit count

A new visitor decides in seconds. Use a clear name and photo, a description that says what they will get and how often, and pin a post that presents your best content.

7. Measure and buy again where it works

Compare the cost per click of each channel where you advertised. Buy again where readers clicked the most, and drop the others. Small channels on the exact topic often beat big general ones.

What to avoid

  • Fake subscribers and bots. They do not read, they lower your view rate and can get your channel restricted.
  • Spam in groups and private messages. It gets you blocked and reported.
  • Giveaways that ask to join many channels. Most of those subscribers leave right after.
