Meme Coin Tycoon FAQ

Meme Coin Tycoon is a free Telegram game where you trade memecoins and real cryptos with virtual money. Launch your own coin, create your own blockchain, mine, stake, join a clan and climb the leaderboard. The trading cash is virtual, but you can earn real TON with the diamond, the weekly contests and your friends.

🎮 Play on Telegram
💡 The dollars, coins and blockchains of the game are virtual money: they cannot be withdrawn. Only the TON rewards (diamond, contests, friends) are real, and they are free: you never pay to win them.

💵 How do I start?

  • You start with $1,000 of virtual cash.
  • Claim 🎁 $200 every day (top of the screen). Day 7 of your streak gives $1,000.
  • Join @memecointycoon: +$500 once, then +$100 more on your daily bonus.
  • Finish the 8 "Getting started" steps and claim $1,500 + 100 $MCT.
  • Do the 3 daily missions, then open the chest.
  • Watch a short video for a free +$500 recharge.

📈 How do prices work?

  • Real cryptos (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Toncoin) follow their real price.
  • Memecoins follow a curve: every buy pushes the price up, every sell pushes it down.
  • Recent coins are the cheapest: early buyers win the most.
  • News (celebrity tweets, whale dumps, hacks) moves the prices.

🚀 How do I launch my own memecoin?

  • Pick a name, a ticker, a logo and a color. It costs $500, up to 3 coins a day.
  • Choose the blockchain: compare its fees and its launch grant.
  • Buy your own coin first to get the lowest price, then share it.
  • You choose your creator fee (0% to 2%, 0.5% by default): you earn it on every buy and sale other players make on your coin.

🎓 What is graduation?

  • A memecoin that passes a $10,000 market cap graduates to the DEX and is announced on the channel.
  • Its creator wins $2,000 once 10 other players hold it, if he still holds some himself.

🚨 What is a rug pull?

When a creator dumps at least half of his own coins, the coin is marked 🚨 Rug for 24 hours and loses its graduation reward. Be careful with coins whose creator sells a lot.

💀 What are dead coins?

Coins with no trade for 7 days, or almost worthless, move to the 💀 Dead tab. You can still buy and sell them, and they come back to the market if they come back to life.

⛓️ What is a blockchain in the game?

  • Every coin lives on a blockchain that takes a fee on each of its trades.
  • Create your own blockchain for $3,000 and set its fee (0.1% to 10%).
  • You earn that fee on every trade of every coin launched on your chain.
  • Optional launch grant: you pay it to each player who launches a coin on your chain, to attract creators.

💸 What fees do I pay on a trade?

  • Memecoins: the creator fee chosen by the creator (shown on the coin) and the fee of its blockchain. When you sell, 2% of the coins sold are burned 🔥, so the price falls less.
  • Real cryptos: 0.5% per buy or sale. $MCT: 2%, shared between its holders every Monday.
  • No creator fee on your own coins.

⛏️ How does mining work?

Buy a rig and pick a coin: it mines while you are away. Upgrade the CPU, GPU and cooling. Like in real life you pay electricity, it gets harder when many players mine the same coin, and rewards halve over time. Collect before your rig overheats.

🔒 What is staking?

Lock coins you hold to get more back: +1% in 1 day, +10% in 7 days, +50% in 30 days. You can unstake early, but then you get no interest.

🎯 Orders and ⚡ futures

  • Limit buy, take-profit, stop-loss and price alerts work even when you are away.
  • Futures: bet on the rise (Long) or the fall (Short) of real cryptos with x2, x5 or x10 leverage. If the loss reaches 90% of your margin, you are liquidated.

🏛️ What is $MCT?

  • The official coin of the game. You can buy, sell, mine and stake it like any coin.
  • Earn it by playing: +5 with your daily bonus (+50 on day 7), +2 per trade of $100 or more (10 a day), +10 per mission and +30 for the chest, +10 per video (3 a day), +100 per friend who really plays, and more for the best clans and the diamond contest.
  • Every Monday, the 2% fees of all $MCT trades are shared between the players who hold at least 100 $MCT.
  • 1,000 $MCT = 🥉 Bronze, 10,000 = 🥈 Silver (+5% mining), 100,000 = 🥇 Gold (+10% mining).

🛡️ Clans

  • Create a clan for $5,000 or join one (20 members at most), and trade together.
  • Every week, the 3 best clans win $MCT for each of their members.

💎 How do I earn real TON?

  • The diamond: tap the small diamond at the top of the game. A new rate every day, and every week the 3 best tappers win $1 / $0.50 / $0.25 in TON.
  • Best players of the week: $1 / $0.50 / $0.25 in TON for the 3 biggest gains (3 trades at least).
  • Friends: $0.05 in TON for every friend who really plays (5 trades and 3 different days of play within 30 days), and $1 / $0.50 / $0.25 for the 3 best inviters of the week.
  • Add your TON wallet on the 💎 TON page. Payouts every Monday, from a $0.10 balance.

🏆 How does the leaderboard work?

Your net worth = your cash + everything you own (coins, staking, open positions). Cash bought with Stars does not count: only what you earn with it counts.

👥 Invite friends

  • You and each friend get $500.
  • Referral contest: $1,000 for the best referrer of the day; $5,000 / $3,000 / $1,000 every week; $15,000 + 👑 crown for 30 days / $7,000 / $3,000 every month.
  • Only friends who make at least one trade count. Prizes are paid automatically (virtual money).
  • Friends who really play also pay you real TON: see above.

⭐ What can I buy with Telegram Stars?

  • ⭐ Cash packs: from +$1,000 to +$7,000 of virtual cash (they do not count in the ranking). A free video gives +$500.
  • 📣 Promote a coin: top of the market for 3 hours + a post on the channel, 25 ⭐ (3 coins at most at the same time).
  • 📣 Promote your blockchain: first in the list for every coin creator for 24 hours + a post, 25 ⭐ (2 at most at the same time).
  • 💎 Supporter pass, mining boosts and streak saves.

Everything is optional: the whole game is free to play.

❓ Is it real money?

The game money is not. All dollars, coins (including $MCT) and blockchains in the game are virtual: they cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for real money, and Stars only buy in-game extras. The TON rewards are real (diamond, weekly contests, friends who really play): they are paid to your TON wallet, and you never need to pay anything to win them.

📒 Where do I see my history?

Portfolio > 📒 Money log: every dollar won and lost, with the reason. Use the filter to see one kind only (buys, sales, bonuses, mining...).
