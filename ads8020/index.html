FAQ

Questions

When does 80/20 Ads launch?

On January 5th, 2027. You can register your channel now so it is approved and ready to earn from day one. Advertisers can join the waitlist.

Who can join as a publisher?

Telegram channels with at least 100 real subscribers. Groups and bots will come later. Each channel is reviewed by hand within 24 to 48 hours.

What does 80/20 mean exactly?

For every ad shown on your channel, you receive 80% of the net amount paid by the advertiser. The platform keeps 20%.

How do I get paid?

Your earnings are tracked in the mini app and can be withdrawn in USDT or TON once you reach the minimum amount.

Do I keep control of my channel?

Yes. You choose the ad categories, the number of ads per day and whether ads are deleted automatically. You can remove the bot at any time.

How does the affiliate program work?

Once your channel is approved, you get a personal link. Every channel or advertiser who signs up with it earns you 15% of the 80/20 Ads commission on their activity, for 12 months. It costs them nothing.
