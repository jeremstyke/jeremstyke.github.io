1. The game

Pass The Baton is a free Telegram game made by Jeremy (t.me/jeremstyke). By playing, you accept these rules.

Each day you can get a baton. While you hold it (up to 4 hours) you earn $BATON. Then you pass it: to a new player (biggest bonus), to someone of your team, to a random player, or in the Club where the first one to tap takes it.

An invite link never takes your baton. Passing is always your choice.

2. Boosts, tasks and videos

A rewarded video gives a x2 boost on your baton for 1 hour.

Tasks (partner channels, sponsored tasks, your name or bio, the Club, daily missions...) give $BATON. A subscription task is paid only if you stay subscribed.

The Last Click: a shared countdown, each click is a video, the last one to click when it ends wins the pot. You must be in the Club and subscribed to @jeremstyke.

3. $BATON and the Shop

$BATON are game points. In the Shop you can exchange them for USDT on the TON network: 1,000,000 $BATON = 1 USDT (1 or 5 USDT per withdrawal).

To withdraw you must have played on at least 5 different days, be a member of the Club @passthebatonclub and be subscribed to @jeremstyke. One withdrawal every 7 days per player. The number of withdrawals per day is limited: when the daily stock is empty, come back the next day.

Payments are sent from the game wallet and announced on @jeremstyke and in the Club. A wrong wallet address cannot be refunded.

4. Referral and partners

You earn 10% of what the players you invited earn, for life.

A group or channel admin who adds the bot becomes a partner: 20%, then 25% and 30% with more players brought.

5. Weekly ranking

Every week the best players win $BATON, and everyone who played that week gets +1,000 $BATON. The ranking is published on @jeremstyke and in the Club.

6. Fair play

One account per person. Multiple accounts (more than 2 on the same connection and phone), bots, scripts, fake referrals or any other cheating: you can keep playing, but you lose any right to real money (Shop and payments). Earnings obtained by cheating can be cancelled.

Insults, spam or scams in the Club or the game can lead to a ban from all our games.

7. Changes and limits

The rewards, rates, tasks and rules can change, be paused or stop at any time, announced in the game or on @jeremstyke. $BATON have no value outside the game and are not an investment.

The game is provided as it is, without guarantee. Telegram, the ad networks and the TON blockchain are third parties.
