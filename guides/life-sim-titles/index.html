Life Sim guide: every life title and how to unlock it

When your life ends in Life Sim, it earns one title that sums it up. Here are all 13 titles, exactly what each one requires, and how to go for the rare ones.

All 13 life titles

The game checks the titles in this order and gives you the first one that matches. Net worth means your cash plus what you own.

Why the order matters: if you are a millionaire with a criminal record, you get The Godfather, not The Millionaire. And if you die in prison, you get Died behind bars, whatever your fortune.

Tips for a legendary life

  • Start early on your gauges. Each gauge rises more slowly as it gets higher, so small daily efforts in childhood pay off.
  • One action per gauge per year. Choose the one that gives the most for your budget.
  • Money makes money. Properties can be rented out every year, and a steady career funds everything else.
  • Watch for rare events. About one year in twenty brings a huge twist: a fortune, fame or a disaster.
  • Write your own move. If no choice fits your plan, type what you want to do: the story adapts.

Frequently asked questions

How many titles are there in Life Sim?

There are 13 life titles, from The Godfather to An Ordinary Life. Each life earns exactly one.

What if my life matches several titles?

The game checks the titles in a fixed order and gives you the first one that matches, which is the order of the table on this page.

How do life goals work?

Each life gets 3 random goals out of 10. They appear under your journal after your first year. Complete one and you get its reward right away, plus a little happiness.
