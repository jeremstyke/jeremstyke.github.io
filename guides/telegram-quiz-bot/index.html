How to add a daily quiz bot to your Telegram group or channel

A quiet group dies slowly. A daily question gives everyone a reason to come back, answer and compare scores. Here is how to set up a free quiz bot in about two minutes.

Why a daily quiz works

People open a group when something new is waiting for them. A question every day at the same time creates a habit, a little competition and plenty of reactions. Your members talk to each other again, and you don't have to write anything yourself.

Set it up in five steps

  1. Open ChatPal and add it to your group. Tap Add ChatPal to a group, pick your group and confirm. For a channel, open your channel settings, go to Administrators and add @mybestfriend_ai_en_bot with the right to post messages.
  2. Pick a theme and a language. ChatPal sends a setup message with buttons. Choose the theme of your questions (football, movies, science, history, general knowledge and more) and the language of your community.
  3. Choose what ChatPal posts. A daily quiz, a topic of the day to spark debates, or both. You can also choose the time of the daily post.
  4. Let it run. Every day, ChatPal posts the question with four answers. Members tap A, B, C or D. The fastest correct answers earn the most points, and answers close after 12 hours.
  5. Watch the leaderboard grow. When answers close, ChatPal posts the right answer, the winners and the leaderboard. Every week, the best player is crowned champion.
Add ChatPal to a group
Tip for channels: Telegram hides the comment bar under any post that has buttons. That is why, in a channel with a discussion group, ChatPal shows the four choices as text in the post and puts the answer buttons in the comments. Your subscribers can still answer in one tap, and discussions stay open.

Tips to get more players

  • Pick a specific theme. A football quiz in a football community gets far more answers than general knowledge.
  • Pin a welcome post that explains the rules: one answer per question, no searching, the fastest earn the most points.
  • Ask members to turn on notifications so they see the question as soon as it is posted.
  • Celebrate the weekly champion with a message or a small reward. Recognition keeps people playing.

Frequently asked questions

Is the ChatPal quiz free?

Yes. Adding ChatPal to your group or channel and running the daily quiz is free.

Does it work in Telegram channels?

Yes. If your channel has a discussion group, the question is posted in the channel and members answer in the comments, so the comments stay open. Without a discussion group, the answer buttons appear right under the post.

Which languages are supported?

ChatPal writes the quiz in the language you choose, including English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian, Russian, Turkish, Arabic, Hindi and Indonesian.

Can members chat with ChatPal in the group?

Yes. Under the topic of the day, ChatPal joins the conversation and replies to comments. Members can also mention it to talk to it.
