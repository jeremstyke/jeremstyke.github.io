Frequently asked questions

What is a Telegram Mini App?

A Mini App is a web app that opens right inside Telegram, from a bot or a link, without installing anything. Games, shops and tools on Telegram are Mini Apps.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe your idea in a sentence and SayApp's AI writes all the code. You then change it the same way, with simple sentences.

How much does it cost?

Your first app is free. After that you use credits: 1 app = 3 credits, 1 change = 1 credit, from 17 ⭐ down to 10 ⭐ per credit, or in USDT. Hosting is free up to 1,000 players a month; above, SayApp Pro is paid automatically with 60 credits a month.

How do I put my app on my own bot?

Right after the AI builds your app, tap "Launch on my bot": Telegram creates your bot in 1 tap and SayApp connects your app, its description, its logo and its welcome message.

Can I earn money with my app?

Yes. Add your own Adsgram ads (interstitials and rewarded videos) and sell items in Telegram Stars, paid straight to your own bot. You can also earn 10% with the affiliate program.

What if a change goes wrong?

Every change can be undone in 1 tap, and a change that fails gives its credit back automatically.

Which languages are supported?

The SayApp studio speaks English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Indonesian and Turkish. Your app can be made in any language, with a main language and extra ones.

Can I pay with crypto?

Yes. Besides Telegram Stars, you can send USDT on the TON network with your personal code: 6 credits per dollar, credited in about 2 minutes.

Is there an affiliate program?

Yes. Open @SayAppBuilderBot, tap "Affiliate program" and join: you earn 10% of every purchase of the people you invite, paid in Stars by Telegram.
