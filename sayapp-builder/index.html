Frequently asked questions

What is a Telegram Mini App?

A Mini App is a web app that opens right inside Telegram, from a bot or a link, without installing anything. Games, shops and tools on Telegram are Mini Apps.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You describe your idea in a sentence and SayApp's AI writes all the code. You then change it the same way, with simple sentences.

How much does it cost?

SayApp Pro: 1,000 ⭐ a month per app (750 ⭐ for life for the first 100 customers). It includes the app built by the AI, your own bot, hosting, updates and 30 changes a month. No commitment.

How do I put my app on my own bot?

Right after paying, the AI builds your app. Then tap "Launch on my bot": Telegram creates your bot in 1 tap and SayApp connects your app, its description, its logo and its welcome message.

Can I earn money with my app?

Yes. Your app can sell bonuses or items in Telegram Stars, and the Stars arrive on your own bot.

What if a change goes wrong?

A failed change is never counted, and you can undo your last change in 1 tap to get back to the previous version.

Which languages are supported?

The SayApp studio speaks English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Indonesian and Turkish. Your app can be made in any language, with a main language and extra ones.
