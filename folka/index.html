Groups

Talk with your family, friends or team. Photos, voice messages, replies, reactions and pinned messages.

Channels

Share news with as many people as you want. Your followers read, react and comment in a linked discussion.

Explore

Find public groups and channels by topic or near you: local news, clubs, associations, hobbies.

No phone number

Sign up with an email. You choose who sees what, and you can leave or mute any group in one tap.

Questions

When will Folka be available?

Folka is in development. It will launch first on iPhone, then on Android shortly after.

Is it free?

Yes. Creating groups, channels and chatting is free.

Do I need a phone number?

No. You sign up with an email address. Your phone number is never asked.

Can I create a channel for my association or my town?

Yes. Anyone can create a public or private channel, and public ones can appear in Explore.
