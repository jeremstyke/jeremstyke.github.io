Snake: Eat, Grow, Don't Bite is the classic Snake game, right inside Telegram. Steer your snake to the fruit, grow a little longer with every bite, and be careful never to hit your own tail.

Each fruit adds to your score, and the longer you survive, the trickier it gets. Play quick rounds, set a new personal best, and see how you rank against other players on the leaderboard.

Features

🍎Eat and grow

Every fruit makes your snake longer and adds to your score.

👆Swipe to turn

Simple swipe controls to steer your snake on your phone.

🌀Passable walls

Walls can be crossed, so you can come out on the other side.

📈Levels

The longer you survive, the trickier the game gets.

📊Your stats

Track your record, games played and fruits eaten.

🏆Leaderboard

See the best snakes and find out where you rank.
