Would You Rather? Crazy Dilemmas is a Telegram mini app full of funny, absurd and sometimes impossible choices. Pick your answer fast, score points and climb the leaderboard.

It is made to be shared. Send your favorite dilemmas to your friends and find out who thinks like you. You can play in English or French.
